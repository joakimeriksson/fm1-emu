Your projects, kept on your computer.

This page talks to a physical FM-1 running SLOOP 3.3.7 over USB. It does not control the emulator. Keep as many project files as you like; load one onto the FM-1 when you need it.

Hardware verification pending. Transfers have automated protocol tests, but have not been tried on a physical FM-1. This first version stores projects only. Samples, user sound banks and firmware are separate.

1. Connect your FM-1

Use Chrome or Edge and a USB data cable. Run SLOOP normally, stop playback and recording, and close other hardware editors. Do not enter bootloader mode.

Not connected.

2. Keep the current project

Save the music as it is now, including unsaved project edits, to a named file on your computer. Your FM-1 is not changed.

Keep these .fm1project files in a folder you back up. They include the firmware version and a checksum. They do not include samples or user sound banks.
